Final Major Project — Nottingham Trent University

Playmakers: Echo the Game

About
Challenge

Explore how football fandom, identity and culture could inform a contemporary sports brand.

Move beyond traditional football aesthetics to create an identity rooted in the behaviour and emotion surrounding the game.

Solution

Developed the Playmakers brand concept through research into football culture, supporters and visual symbolism.

Created a visual identity spanning branding, typography, editorial design, campaign imagery and apparel.

Designed a matchday programme-inspired publication combining football storytelling with a contemporary editorial approach.

Outcome

Produced a complete sports identity demonstrating how fan culture can shape brand storytelling.

The project became the culmination of my final-year work exploring the relationship between sport, design and identity.

Live Client Work — Mendip Spring Golf & Country Club

The Green Mile

About
Challenge

Transform Mendip Spring's challenging four-hole stretch into a distinctive, recognisable experience.

Create something that could engage existing members while encouraging competition, sharing and repeat play.

Solution

Developed The Green Mile visual identity and wider creative direction.

Designed a system spanning course signage, digital graphics, social content and motion.

Designed and developed an interactive scoring website and leaderboard to extend the experience beyond the course.

Built the identity around the idea of golfers attempting to “conquer” The Green Mile.

Outcome

Created a cohesive experience connecting physical, digital and social touchpoints.

The project has developed into an ongoing relationship with Mendip Spring, expanding into further design work for the club.

Dissertation — Research

Football Fandom

About
The Question

How does football fandom influence identity, fashion and visual culture?

Explored why supporters use clothing, symbols and shared rituals to communicate belonging.

The Research

Investigated the relationship between football, fashion, symbolism and supporter identity.

Explored fan behaviour and the emotional connection between clubs, communities and individuals.

Used the research to understand how visual codes within football can communicate belonging and cultural identity.

The Insight

Identified how football extends beyond the match itself into fashion, community, lifestyle and self-expression.

These insights directly informed the development of Playmakers and my approach to designing for sports audiences.

Design Competition — Outerwear

Paul Smith: Cool in the Cold

About
The Brief

Develop an outerwear concept responding to a live Paul Smith design brief.

Create a distinctive collection balancing function, storytelling and contemporary design.

The Concept — Cool in the Cold

Inspired by extreme mountaineering, from historic polar expeditions to Nimsdai’s record-breaking 14 Peaks challenge.

Drew on my own experiences in mountain environments and exposure to technical cold-weather clothing.

Translated the functionality and visual language of expedition equipment into a considered outerwear collection.

Combined technical influences with a more refined, contemporary approach suited to Paul Smith.

Recognition

Selected as a finalist for the Paul Smith live brief.

Developed the project from initial research and concept through to a resolved outerwear proposal.
