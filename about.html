About Me.

I’m a multidisciplinary designer focused on sport, branding and digital experiences. A recent Nottingham Trent University graduate, I’m particularly interested in how design can shape the way people connect with sport, brands and culture.

Since graduating, I’ve been working with Mendip Spring Golf Club across a growing range of creative projects, from developing The Green Mile brand identity and interactive scoring experience to digital content, motion and on-course graphics. Before this, I spent a year working professionally as an Assistant Designer in Hamburg, gaining hands-on commercial experience across design, product development and visual communication.

Sport has always been a huge part of my life. Having competed across golf, rugby and cricket, that experience has naturally shaped the work I’m interested in creating and my understanding of the audiences I’m designing for.

I’m now looking to continue building my career within sports creative, working across branding, digital design, campaigns, social content and motion.
